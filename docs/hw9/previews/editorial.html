One workspace.
Five connected capabilities.

For programme managers, field coordinators, and monitoring and evaluation teams. Bring responsibilities, reporting information, and indicator evidence into a clearer view.

Project management

Organize project work, assign responsibility, set deadlines, and follow tasks through review.

Monitoring

Follow operational progress and reporting records. See what has been submitted and what still needs attention.

Indicators

Examine baselines, targets, and results. For configured indicators, inspect the selected method and reporting period.

Indicator analysis requires approved methods and suitable source data.

Analytics

Understand supported indicator calculations through explanations, source context, and data-quality checks.

Checks apply to the selected indicator. Missing configuration and unsupported results are identified explicitly.

Reporting

Review recorded information and prepare report previews for discussion and decisions.

Fully governed report and export snapshots remain future work.

Know what the result
can tell you.

Institutional decisions need more than a completed-task count. Delivery checks and indicator evidence answer different questions.

Work completed.

A completed task shows that the work passed its delivery checks. Responsibilities, deadlines, progress, and review checkpoints describe what happened.

Results understood.

Configured indicators offer a separate view of what the data shows. Examine the method, period, source context, and limitations before interpreting a result.

  • Keep task progress distinct from measured outcomes.
  • Recognize when a calculation is unavailable.
  • Use reporting records and previews to support review.
