  - region "Work you can trace. Evidence you can explain.":
    - heading "Work you can trace. Evidence you can explain." [level=2]
    - paragraph: Delivery checks and indicator measurements answer different questions. Keep both in view when discussing results.
    - paragraph: Illustrative examples of supported behavior — not live project results or application screenshots.
    - article:
      - heading "Submitted is not yet approved." [level=3]
      - paragraph: A coordinator submits a completed field activity. In the two-stage workflow, a reviewer validates it before the manager approves completion.
      - term: Submission
      - definition: The work is ready for review.
      - term: Validation
      - definition: The submission passes the review checkpoint.
      - term: Approval
      - definition: The final completion checkpoint is confirmed.
      - paragraph: These states describe delivery. They do not, by themselves, demonstrate an institutional outcome.
    - article:
      - heading "A result needs its context." [level=3]
      - paragraph: "For a method that measures change from a baseline to a final target, these illustrative inputs give 50% achievement:"
      - term: Baseline
      - definition: "20"
      - term: Reported value
      - definition: "60"
      - term: Final target
      - definition: "100"
      - paragraph:
        - text: Change toward the final target
        - strong: (60 − 20) ÷ (100 − 20) × 100 = 50%
      - paragraph: In RC Atlas, a supported calculation is tied to its configured method, source data, period, and version. This percentage is not task completion or proof of impact; another method can answer a different question.
    - paragraph: Reporting brings recorded information and previews into the review conversation. Interpret each result alongside its method and limitations.
