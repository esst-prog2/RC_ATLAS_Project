## Inventory boundaries and difference explanations

The canonical engine (`services/methodology.py`) is the single authority, not a
second consumer engine. Its definitions/formulas were not edited. Source-only
helpers which create/find/link indicators, sanitize layouts, persist templates,
register routes or generate synthetic seed fixtures are covered as inputs or
plumbing in C42–45, not counted again as analytical results. Test fixtures,
archived OpenSpec documents, academic `spike/` material and unrelated projects are
not runtime consumers. No original untracked file was examined.

Location achievement used to be inferred from baseline presence and then averaged
or target-weighted. It is now unavailable until a governed source binding exists.
For the approved fictional bound dataset, actual60/baseline20/target100 is exactly
50%, independently of locations or reported history percentages. That difference
is an explicit change of authority/population, not an attempt to reproduce the old
aggregate. Final achievement50% and planned-target attainment100% are different
named metrics, with distinct identities and identical supported consumer behavior.

The old quality composite mixed missingness, duplication, arbitrary age penalties
and outlier penalties. OC-A01 prohibits that universal score. Its replacement has
five rule-specific dimensions; no rule or assessable population yields
NOT_ASSESSED. Missing observations remain missing; zero remains numeric zero.

No inferred record filtering, version selection, calendar cutoff, currency
conversion, fitness threshold or organization profile was introduced. Selected
consumer queries reject unknown/repeated parameters and conflicting URL/query
scope. Historical records with unresolved ownership are neither repaired nor
used as approved observations.
