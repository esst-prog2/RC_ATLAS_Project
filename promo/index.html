One workspace.
Five connected capabilities.

For programme managers, field coordinators, and monitoring and evaluation teams in institutions and project organizations.

A completed-task count cannot explain the whole picture. Teams also need clear responsibilities, reporting records, and evidence they can interpret.

Project management

Organize project work, assign responsibility, set deadlines, and follow tasks through review.

Monitoring

Follow operational progress and reporting records. See what has been submitted and what still needs attention.

Indicators

Examine baselines, targets, and results. For configured indicators, inspect the selected method and reporting period.

Analysis requires approved methods and suitable source data.

Analytics

Understand supported indicator calculations through explanations, source context, and data-quality checks.

Checks apply to the selected indicator. Missing configuration and unsupported results are identified explicitly.

Reporting

Review recorded information and prepare report previews to support discussion and decisions.

Final report exports with a preserved audit record remain future work.

Accountability,
one handoff at a time.

Work you can trace.
Evidence you can explain.

Delivery checks and indicator measurements answer different questions. Keep both in view when discussing results.

Illustrative examples of supported behavior — not live project results or application screenshots.

Submitted is not yet approved.

A coordinator submits a completed field activity. In the two-stage workflow, a reviewer validates it before the manager approves completion.

Submission
The work is ready for review.
Validation
The submission passes the review checkpoint.
Approval
The final completion checkpoint is confirmed.

These states describe delivery. They do not, by themselves, demonstrate an institutional outcome.

A result needs its context.

For a method that measures change from a baseline to a final target, these illustrative inputs give 50% achievement:

Baseline
20
Reported value
60
Final target
100

Change toward the final target(60 − 20) ÷ (100 − 20) × 100 = 50%

In RC Atlas, a supported calculation is tied to its configured method, source data, period, and version. This percentage is not task completion or proof of impact; another method can answer a different question.

Reporting brings recorded information and previews into the review conversation. Interpret each result alongside its method and limitations.
